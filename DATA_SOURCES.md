# Data Sources & Attribution

This tracker uses ECMWF IFS, IFS Ensemble (ENS), and AIFS forecast data.

ECMWF data are provided under the applicable ECMWF Open Data licence,
including CC BY 4.0 where applicable.

Source:
https://www.ecmwf.int/

## NOAA / NCEP

The tracker uses NOAA/NCEP AI-GFS forecast data.

Source:
https://www.ncep.noaa.gov/

## NOAA / NCEI

The tracker uses NOAA/NCEI OISST v2.1 sea-surface temperature data.

Source:
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/

This tracker is an independent visualization and processing of
weather-model and ocean-data products.

It is not an official forecast or warning.

For official forecasts, warnings and advisories, follow IMD and
the relevant government meteorological authorities.

Data remain subject to the terms and licences of their respective
data providers.
